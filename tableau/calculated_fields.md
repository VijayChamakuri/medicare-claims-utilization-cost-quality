## Filters

- **Year:** every year-based sheet keeps rows where `In selected year` is true, so one Year control drives all pages.
- **Peer group:** Provider Operations sheets share one filter on `provider_type` (default: inpatient facilities).
- **Race and sex:** the demographic sheet on Utilization & Payment has its own quick filters.
- **Drill-through:** selecting facilities on the Provider Operations scatter filters the review queue by provider ID.
