## Dashboards

1. **Executive Overview:** KPI tiles, monthly paid amount by setting, claims per 1,000 members.
2. **Utilization & Payment:** paid per beneficiary, claim and admission; paid by setting; concentration curve; demographic and risk-tier views.
3. **Provider Operations:** peer-group scatter, flags by reason and a review queue that filters from the scatter.
4. **Quality & Cohorts:** readmission proxy with numerator, denominator and exclusions; risk tiers; top conditions; chronic condition flags.
5. **Data Quality & Definitions:** reconciliation status and detail, data-quality counts, source manifest, metric definitions.
